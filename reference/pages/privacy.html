Information we collect

We collect the data you provide when you contact us or use our tools — such as your name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, company, website URL, and the message or website you submit to our free AI-Visibility and SEO checks. We also collect standard technical data automatically: IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, and usage analytics.

How we use your information (purposes)

Primary purposes: to respond to your enquiries, deliver the report or action plan you request, provide and improve our services and tools, and fulfil legal and security obligations. Secondary purposes (which you may opt out of at any time): sending you relevant updates, content, and commercial communications. Opting out of secondary purposes does not affect the services you requested.

Cookies & analytics

With your consent, we use cookies and third-party analytics and marketing tools — including Google Analytics 4, Search Console, Tag Manager, the Meta and TikTok advertising pixels, and Microsoft Clarity (which records anonymized session activity such as clicks, scrolls, and mouse movement) — to understand how the site is used, measure our marketing, and improve your experience. The Meta and TikTok pixels and Microsoft Clarity load only after you accept via the cookie banner. Google Analytics 4 loads on every visit under Google Consent Mode: until you accept, it sets no cookies and collects no identifiers, sending only anonymous, aggregated signals; accepting enables full measurement. You can change your decision at any time from the "Cookies" link in the footer, and you can also disable or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some features may not work as intended without them.

Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers (encargados) that help us operate — hosting, analytics, email, and communication platforms — strictly to provide these services and under appropriate safeguards. Some providers may process data outside Mexico. We may also disclose data when required by a competent authority or by law.

Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described here and any applicable legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.
