About

Formerly Kainos MX

Always evolving. Never obsolete.

Technology never sits still — so neither do we. For 15+ years we've helped businesses run better on tech, reinventing how we work with every project, so what we build never falls behind.

Our story

It started with a single website.

We began as Kainos MX, a small studio shipping websites for whoever needed one. Every project taught us something new — so we said yes to harder problems, then harder ones again.

Websites became platforms. Platforms became the systems that run entire companies — for startups and SMBs and transnationals alike. The lesson stuck: say yes, then figure out the how. There's no problem in technology we won't take on.

Somewhere in that climb the old name stopped fitting. We weren't a web studio anymore — we were the team a company calls when something core has to change. So we became WeEvolveIT, and built around the one constant we'd learned: never stand still. That's the real shift: we don't sell technology like a commodity. We plug in as a part of your company — the team whose job is to know every process and need of your business, and keep you current as the ground keeps moving.
