What is nearshore software development?

Nearshore software development is outsourcing software work to a team in a nearby country that shares most of your working hours. For US companies, that often means Mexico. WeEvolveIT is a nearshore software development company in Monterrey whose senior engineers work as part of your team.

Nearshore vs. offshore: what's the difference?
Offshore teams, like those in India, run 9.5 to 13.5 hours ahead of the lower 48 states, so each question waits a day. Nearshore teams share your workday.
Nearshore vs. in-house hiring?
In-house means US salaries and long recruiting cycles. Nearshore gives you vetted senior engineers on your hours, at a lower cost.

Key takeaways

  • Nearshore = a senior team on your business hours, not offshore teams working while you sleep.

FAQ

What teams ask before going nearshore.

01What is nearshore software development?

It means hiring a software team in a nearby country, a few hours or less from your time zone, instead of one across the world. You collaborate in real time, unlike offshore, and usually pay less than for the same seniority in the US.

02How much does nearshore software development cost?

Our rates start at $50 USD per hour and scale with seniority and scope. Staff augmentation is billed per engineer each month, dedicated teams at a monthly squad rate, and full projects against milestones. Every engagement starts with a fixed-price discovery.

03What nearshore software development services do you offer?

Three engagement models: staff augmentation (senior engineers embedded in your team), dedicated teams (a pod with engineering, QA, design and PM) and nearshore custom software development (a full project from discovery to launch). All three work your hours and let you approve each engineer.

04How do I choose a nearshore software development company?

Check four things: shared working hours, senior engineers you interview yourself, transparent pricing and clear IP ownership. WeEvolveIT works from Monterrey with 8 to 9 hours of overlap with a US Central workday, 15+ years, 1,000+ projects and a 5.0 rating on Google and Trustindex.

05Why choose Mexico for nearshore software development?

Because of the clock. Monterrey stays on UTC-6 all year: the same time as Dallas and Chicago from November to March, one hour behind them from March to November. That leaves at least 7 shared hours with any contiguous US time zone, so questions get same-day answers.

06Do we own the code and the IP?

Yes. You own all the code and intellectual property from day one. The engineers work as part of your team, inside your process; you interview and approve every person, and you always know who is working on your product. No black-box resources.

07Staff augmentation or a dedicated team: which do I need?

Choose staff augmentation if you have engineering leadership and need more senior hands inside your process. Choose a dedicated team if you want a complete pod (engineering, QA, design and PM) that owns a product or workstream end to end. Both are billed monthly.

08How fast can a nearshore team start?

We confirm the start date in the discovery, based on the roles and stack you need. We pre-vet senior engineers for your stack, you interview them, and the team joins your tools and stand-ups from week one, with no recruiting on your side.
