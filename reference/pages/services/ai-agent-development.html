What does an AI agent development company do?

An AI agent development company designs, builds, and maintains AI agents: software that uses a language model to plan toward a goal, call your tools and APIs, and complete work end to end, such as resolving tickets or qualifying leads. WeEvolveIT does this from Monterrey, Mexico, building custom agents that hold up in production.

AI agent vs chatbot vs automation?
A chatbot answers with text and stops there. Automation runs fixed rules: reliable, but it can't handle anything off the script. An AI agent reasons, uses your tools, and acts, so it issues the refund a chatbot could only explain.

Key takeaways

  • Agents act, chatbots reply: an agent plans, uses your tools, and completes the task.

  • Built for production: guardrails, evals, and monitoring before and after launch.

  • Wired into your stack: ERP, CRM, help desk, and data via API, on the model that fits.

  • Senior team in Monterrey: UTC-6 all year, 1,000+ projects in 17 countries over 15+ years, rated 5.0 on Google and Trustindex.

FAQ

What teams ask about AI agents.

01How do I choose an AI agent development company?

Choose one that ships agents to production, not demos. Ask for evals that prove task success before launch, real integrations with your ERP and CRM, and guardrails such as least-privilege access and human approval. Prefer senior engineers and a scoped, fixed plan. WeEvolveIT brings all of that, with 1,000+ projects delivered across 17 countries.

02What's included in your AI agent development services?

The full build: scoping the workflow and what 'done' means, connecting the agent to your tools via API, designing its planning, memory, and retrieval, adding guardrails and evals, and launching with logging and monitoring. We build autonomous agents for support, sales, operations, research, and internal teams, and keep tuning them after launch.

03What can AI agents do for a business?

AI agents take on repeatable work that used to need a person: resolving support tickets, qualifying and following up on leads, running operations workflows, preparing reports, and answering internal questions. They work inside your ERP, CRM, help desk, and databases, with a person approving sensitive steps. The best first agent targets one clear, repetitive bottleneck.

04How much does AI agent development cost?

Cost depends on scope, the number of systems the agent touches, and the guardrails the workflow needs. A single pilot agent costs far less than a multi-agent platform. We start with a discovery, then give you a fixed number before we build anything. Our senior engineers bill from $50 USD per hour.

05Can you integrate an agent with our ERP, CRM, and tools?

Yes, and integration is most of the work. We connect agents to ERPs like SAP and NetSuite, CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho, plus help desks, databases, and internal tools, via API or custom connectors with least-privilege access. The agent acts inside the stack you already run; it doesn't replace it.

06How do you keep AI agents under control in production?

With AI agent governance built in from day one. Each agent gets least-privilege access to only the tools it needs, validation on its outputs, human approval for sensitive actions, and evals before launch. We can deploy in your own cloud, such as AWS Bedrock or Azure AI, so your data stays in your environment, and we monitor it after launch.

07Which frameworks and models do you build AI agents with?

We're framework- and model-agnostic. We use LangGraph for stateful, multi-step agents, CrewAI for crews of specialized agents, AutoGen for conversational, tool-using agents, and n8n to orchestrate workflows. Models include OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, and Llama, chosen per task for quality, cost, and data privacy, including open models that run on your own infrastructure.

08Do you build AI agents for companies in the United States?

Yes. WeEvolveIT is an AI agent development company headquartered in Monterrey, Mexico, building custom agents for US companies and integrating them with the systems they already run. Monterrey keeps UTC-6 year-round, so we share the Dallas and Chicago clock from November to March and run one hour behind them from March to November.
